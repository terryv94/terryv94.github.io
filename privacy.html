Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

nest-homebridge is a personal integration used only by its owner to control their own Google Nest devices from Apple Home via a self-hosted Homebridge server.

What data is accessed

With the owner's authorization, the integration reads device information and status (such as temperature, humidity, mode and setpoints) and sends control commands through Google's Smart Device Management API. It receives device events through Google Cloud Pub/Sub.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to display and control the owner's devices in Apple Home. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored only on the owner's private Homebridge server. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account's security settings or the Google Home app's partner connections.
